Background & Philosophy

A grounded approach to data & depth.

I grew up in Siwan, Bihar, and recently moved to Lucknow to pursue my Master of Computer Applications at Integral University. Coming from a small town as the eldest daughter, responsibility has always been my compass—not as a burden, but as a steady motivation. For me, education is not just about earning another degree; it is the deliberate path toward financial independence, giving my parents peace of mind, and ensuring my younger siblings have every opportunity to pursue their own dreams.

My analytical foundation took shape during my BCA, where I graduated with a 9.6 CGPA. While academic rigor taught me discipline, my real curiosity ignited when I built my first end-to-end Machine Learning system: predicting breast cancer prognostic outcomes using Support Vector Machines. That project showed me what data truly represents—how noisy clinical measurements, when structured through clean SQL pipelines and validated models, can translate into decisions that genuinely matter to human lives.

Right now, I spend my evenings diving into the AI/ML Prime Batch at Apna College, learning the mechanics of Deep Learning, Neural Networks, GANs, and Generative AI. I do my best work when presented with a clear problem and the autonomy to think through the solution from first principles. I am honest about where I am growing—stepping forward in public speaking and preparing for my first formal industry role—because real capability comes from doing the quiet, focused work every single day.
